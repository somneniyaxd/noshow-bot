Via Telegram. You add a booking → the bot gives you a link → the client opens it once — and gets all reminders automatically.

Then remind them yourself — via WhatsApp or a call. The bot just does not send a reminder.

No. Everything works inside Telegram. No apps, no sites, no signups.

An instant notification: "Anna cancelled the visit at 18:00". You see the slot is free right away.

After 10 minutes the bot sends you a message: "Anna hasn't replied". You can then call her yourself.
